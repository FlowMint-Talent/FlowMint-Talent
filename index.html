How It Works

Every search starts
with your brief.
Not our archive.

We don’t recycle. Every assignment is approached as if the right person doesn’t exist yet — because often, for your specific situation, they haven’t been found yet.

1
Brief

We listen before we look

Before a single approach is made, we take time to understand the role properly — the team, the culture, the technical environment and the honest reasons this is a compelling move for the right person. That depth shapes everything that follows.

2
Search

Targeted. Discreet. Direct.

We identify the specific people worth approaching through market mapping and long-term relationship building — not keyword searches. We reach out directly, with a tailored conversation, to professionals who are performing well and only open to the right move. That’s a fundamentally different pool of talent to what a job posting reaches.

3
Qualify

You see fewer people. Better ones.

Every candidate is assessed properly before they reach you — their technical background, their motivations, their fit with your team and what they genuinely want next. You receive a shortlist with our written rationale for each person. Not volume. The right people, with a clear explanation of why.

4
Manage

Both sides, all the way through

We stay close from first conversation to signed offer — briefing candidates before each stage, relaying honest feedback and keeping exceptional people engaged. In a market where great candidates have options, how you run a process is part of your employer brand.

5
Placed

Placed. And supported.

We advise on offer structure, manage the acceptance and stay in contact after the start date — making sure both sides are happy well beyond day one.
