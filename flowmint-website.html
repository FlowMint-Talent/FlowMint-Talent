Sole trader electrician · 15–20 enquiries/week · No admin support · Uses Gmail and a basic website

Spark Package · £299/mo Upgraded to Flow after 6 weeks

"I was losing jobs because I simply forgot to chase quotes. I didn't realise how much money was walking out the door. FlowMint fixed it in a week and I've not thought about it since."

— Dave S., DS Electrical

The Problem

Dave was sending 15–20 quotes per week by email, then doing nothing to follow them up. Between jobs, he had no time to chase. He estimated he was losing 4–5 jobs a month to silence — customers who would have booked but simply moved on because no one chased them. He also had zero Google reviews despite years of happy customers, because he never got around to asking.

What FlowMint Built

1

Quote Follow-Up Sequence

Every sent quote triggers an automatic follow-up at day 2 (email), day 5 (SMS), and day 10 (final email) — all written by AI in Dave's own voice. Dave does nothing.

2

Instant Enquiry Reply

Anyone who fills in his contact form gets a personalised reply within 60 seconds, even when Dave is on a job at 7pm. Dave gets a summary SMS so he can call back when ready.

3

Google Review Request

When a job is marked complete, the customer automatically receives a thank-you SMS with a direct Google review link. Dave went from 6 to 34 reviews in 3 months.

4

EICR Renewal Reminders

Certificate expiry dates are tracked automatically. Customers get reminder emails at 90, 30, and 7 days before expiry — with a booking link. Recurring revenue Dave never had to ask for.
